# ADR-010 — AI-provider boundary

Status: **Deferred**

Date: 2026-08-17

Production AI Coach is out of Training Core scope. No AI SDK, prompt framework, model adapter, or AI data schema is authorized during the current program.

When Coach is later considered, it must consume explicit FORGE capabilities/context through a provider-neutral adapter and must not become the canonical source of training records.

Owner approval required before AI scope is introduced.
